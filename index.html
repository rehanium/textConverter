Features

📝 Text to Quoted List

Convert multi-line text into quoted, comma-separated values perfect for SOQL IN clauses and Apex collections.

🔄 Record ID Converter

Automatically detects and converts Salesforce Record IDs between 15 and 18 character formats with one click.

🎯 Deduplicate & Sort

Remove duplicate values and sort alphabetically (A→Z or Z→A) for cleaner, organized data.

📦 Wrapper Templates

Automatically wrap output in parentheses () for SOQL or braces {} for Apex collections.

✂️ Delimiter Splitting

Split comma, tab, or custom delimited input into separate lines before processing.

⚠️ SOQL Limit Warning

Visual alert when output exceeds 4,000 characters (Salesforce SOQL query limit).

🔍 Smart Statistics

Track line count, unique values, character count, and automatically detect Record IDs in your input.

⚙️ Customization Options

Single/double quotes, newline/space separation, whitespace trimming, empty line handling, and quote escaping.
